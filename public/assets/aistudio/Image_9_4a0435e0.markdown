## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance cyber defense posture for real-time scam detection, digital identity verification, and anti-fraud monitoring. Built for security analysts, incident responders, and high-risk digital users, the visual personality projects uncompromising authority, split-second clarity, and military-grade precision. 

The aesthetic synthesizes tactical terminal brutalism with surgical telemetry ergonomics:
- **Terminal Brutalism:** Exacting zero-radius geometric geometry, hairline technical borders, and high-density monospaced data grids.
- **Threat Vector Hierarchy:** Visual alarms leverage high-visibility alert pigments directly mapped to risk velocity—from neutral telemetry baselines up to critical zero-day scam interception.
- **Defensive Utility:** Elimination of decorative blur or soft organic shapes; every UI element functions as an instrumentation readout, tactical trigger, or defense status indicator.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around dark tactical surfaces and an indexed threat severity scale:

- **Primary Alert Anchor (`#F94144`):** Intercept Red. Reserved strictly for critical severity threats, active fraud execution, emergency defensive stops, and live scam blocks.
- **Secondary Structural Accent (`#3C6E71`):** Deep Cyber Teal. Used for secondary telemetry controls, verified protocol states, active scanner headers, and secondary badges.
- **Tertiary Safe Anchor (`#43AA8B`):** Hardened Green. Signals verified clean status, zero detected vectors, certified cryptographic hashes, and live operational health.
- **Neutral Surface Foundation (`#353535`):** Carbon Charcoal. Anchor for terminal background canvases, structural card surfaces, command headers, and baseline data tables.

### Threat Progression & Auxiliary Accents
- **Level 4 Critical:** `#F94144` (Breach / Impersonation Scam Detected)
- **Level 3 Severe:** `#F3722C` (High Probability Phishing Pattern)
- **Level 2 Warning:** `#F8961E` / `#F9844A` (Suspicious Network / Unverified Sender)
- **Level 1 Caution:** `#F9C74F` (Elevated Risk Score / Domain Inconsistency)
- **Level 0 Safe:** `#90BE6D` / `#43AA8B` (Encrypted / Verified Entity)
- **Telemetry & Infrastructure:** `#4D908E` (System Telemetry), `#577590` (Diagnostic Inactive), `#277DA1` (Network Uplink), `#284B63` (Deep Tactical Infill).
- **Text & Surface Boundaries:** Pure high-visibility typography via `#FFFFFF`, subtle rule dividers and secondary text in `#D9D9D9`.

## Typography

The type system balances technical urgency with tabular legibility:
- **Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Engineered for rapid tactical ingestion. Space Grotesk introduces structured geometric precision to section titles, critical alerts, and global intercept counters.
- **Telemetry & Body (JetBrains Mono):** Designed for uninterrupted reading of threat code, IP logs, DNS records, token balances, and system messages. The fixed-width pitch guarantees tabular alignment across high-throughput data streams.
- **Labels & Micro-Data:** Always rendered in uppercase monospaced text with tight vertical bounding boxes to mirror radar displays and hardware consoles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a strict, non-elastic 12-column terminal grid:
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer margins. Modules prioritize simultaneous multi-stream readouts (e.g., Threat Monitor + Live Feed + Hash Verification side-by-side).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 6 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Threat streams stack into vertical telemetry panels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `0.75rem` gutters and `1rem` margins. Data feeds collapse into full-width cards with locked monospaced action bars pinned to viewport anchors.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows compact mathematical intervals (`0.25rem` base) to maintain dense information capacity without clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects ambient soft drop-shadows and glass blurs in favor of architectural **Tonal Layers** and **Crisp Structural Borders**:

1. **Base Layer (Canvas):** Pure `#1A1D20` or `#212529` ground plane.
2. **Surface Tier 1 (Containers & Panels):** Inset surfaces tinted `#353535` with solid `1px` outer borders tinted `#284B63` or `#577590`.
3. **Surface Tier 2 (Elevated Modals & Alerts):** `#284B63` solid background bordered by `#D9D9D9` at 40% opacity, accented with a 2px top edge indicator in `#F94144` or `#43AA8B`.
4. **Active/Armed Layer (Interactive State):** Hard offset drop (2px 2px 0px `#000000`) creating a mechanical stepped surface rather than an ambient glow.

## Shapes

The design system enforces strict zero-radius (`0px`) sharp geometry across every component:
- **Borders & Shells:** Crisp 90-degree squared corners for buttons, input fields, notification badges, status pills, and tactical cards.
- **Cut Corners (Accent Option):** Interactive defense controls may optionally use a 45-degree chamfered corner (`4px` clip-path) on top-right edges to signify executable interception tools.
